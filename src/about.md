## Who I am

I'm an electronic technician by training (graduated with honors, 2022 — which surprised even me, given how sick I was). Before that, I spent seven or eight years at [CCP Games](https://www.ccpgames.com/) in Reykjavík, where I went from volunteer bughunter to QA to **Senior Virtual World Database Administrator** — the person keeping TQ, EVE Online's player economy database, alive.

I spent about a year working with a Microsoft escalation engineer on a rare SQL Server crash, which turned into several official Microsoft hotfixes. I've been deep in database internals ever since.

## What I tinker with

- **Rust and hobby OS development** — TheseusOS is my long-running x86_64 kernel project.
- **Reverse engineering and systems programming** — decades of interest, mostly x86.
- **FPGAs and low-level hardware** — NES emulation in Verilog, GSM experiments, and other archaeology.

## Where I am

I've been living in Vejle, Denmark for a few years now, having moved to be closer to my daughter's healthcare. Icelandic is my native tongue, and I read the other Nordic languages (Finnish is beyond me — everyone's got a limit).
